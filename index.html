Visiting McLeod Ganj from Abroad?

We understand international travel timelines. Here is how we ensure seamless consultation, high-altitude healing, and hassle-free booking during your Himachal stay.

01

The Triund Trek Protocol

Planning to hike Triund or Kareri Lake? We advise getting tattooed after your high-altitude trek. Intense mountain UV sun and heavy sweating can stress fresh ink. Doing your session post-trek ensures flawless, serene recovery.

Pre-Arrival Remote Consultations

Traveling from London, Barcelona, Berlin, or NYC? Send your ideas and references on WhatsApp before boarding your flight or while in Delhi. Our artist will sketch and finalize your concept so your slot is reserved the day you arrive.
